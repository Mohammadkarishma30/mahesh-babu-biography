Career

Mahesh Babu is a prominent actor and producer in Telugu cinema (Tollywood). • Full Name: Ghattamaneni Mahesh Babu; born 9 August 1975 in Chennai (then Madras) to veteran actor Krishna. • Started as a child actor from age 4 (in the film Needa, 1979) and appeared in several films in his youth. • Made his debut as a lead actor in the film Rajakumarudu (1999) and won the Nandi Award for Best Male Debut for it. • Over the years, he has delivered many commercial successes and has established himself as one of the highest paid actors in South India. • He has also ventured into film production and philanthropy. Key Phases & Milestones Early lead‐actor period (1999–2003): • With Rajakumarudu (1999) he emerged as a “hero”. • His films like Murari (2001) and Okkadu (2003) brought him bigger recognition; Okkadu in particular won him the Filmfare Award for Best Actor – Telugu. Commercial success & stardom (2004–2014): • Films like Athadu (2005), Pokiri (2006) cemented his mass‐hero status. • In 2011, Dookudu became a major hit and crossed the 100 crore mark (a big benchmark in Telugu cinema). • Films like Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu (2013) – a multi-starrer – showcased his versatility and commercial appeal. Recent years (2015 onward): • Srimanthudu (2015) was also produced by him, focusing on rural development themes.
